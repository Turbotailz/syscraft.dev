#title
Welcome

#description
Syscraft is a community for everyone involved in the Minecraft server community to gather, ask for help, share stories, and make the server world a better place to hang out!

You're welcome as an admin, moderator, developer, owner, host, or even as a player interested in how it all works!

Contributing

#description
